Chapter II

About Me

❦

I am Boyang Cheng. I received the B.S. degree from Southeast University, Nanjing, China, in 2020, and did my graduate study in Electrical Engineering at the University of Notre Dame from 2022 to 2026. My research lies at the intersection of digital and analog/mixed-signal circuit design, with a focus on developing energy-efficient and explainable/trustworthy hardware systems: compute-in-entropy, hardware security with physical unclonable functions (PUFs), and circuits for trustworthy biomedical and autonomous systems. During graduate school I interned at Samsung, working on SRAM design in 2024 and on design technology co-optimization (DTCO) in 2025. I am now a Senior Engineer at Samsung Semiconductor.

With a background in both digital and analog/mixed-signal design, I enjoy tackling challenges that require cross-domain thinking and collaboration. My long-term goal is to advance hardware platforms that empower the next generation of intelligent, reliable, and resource-efficient technologies.

Fun fact I am the first student of my advisor.
